How results are calculated · Estimated from available records

100 profit equals 1 unit. Figures use the supplied per-pick profit where readable. Where profit is absent, moneyline picks use a 1-unit convention. Nineteen picks without visible odds are estimated at -110, risking 1.10u to win 1u, and marked with an asterisk (*). All outcomes are taken from the supplied records.
