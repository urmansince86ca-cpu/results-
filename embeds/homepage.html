Quick answers

Know what you’re buying.

Can I see a free sports pick before joining?

Yes. Visit our Free Sports Picks page to see posted selections and their status. Free picks are published when available. Full All Sports VIP access is delivered through our private Telegram channel.

What is the difference between one-time and auto-renew?

A one-time pass charges you once and ends after the stated access period. An auto-renew subscription charges you again every 7 or 30 days, depending on your selected plan, until canceled.

Do all plans include the same picks?

Yes. All plans include the same All Sports VIP service during your active access period, with posted odds and 1–3 unit ratings. Player props and specialty plays are included when available.

Why is 30-day access $99 or $119?

The $99 plan automatically renews every 30 days until canceled. The $119 pass is a single payment for 30 days with no automatic renewal.

Where do I receive picks?

Picks are delivered in the private VIP Telegram channel. Follow the instructions provided after checkout and turn on notifications. Releases vary by day and sport.

Which plan should I start with?

The $12.99 one-day pass lets you try 24 hours of VIP access with no automatic renewal. Choose longer access when you are ready.

How can I get help with access or billing?

Email members@professorspicks.com for help with Telegram access, billing, or cancellation.

Does VIP guarantee profit?

No. Picks can lose and odds can change. Past results do not guarantee future performance.
